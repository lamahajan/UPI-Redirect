Last updated: 13 September 2026

The terms that apply when you use DigiFee Mitra to check or pay a student's school fees online.

01Acceptance of terms

By using DigiFee Mitra to look up or pay school fees, you agree to these Terms & Conditions. If you do not agree, please contact the School office to pay fees by an alternative method.

02About the portal

DigiFee Mitra is an official online portal of Lotus International School that lets a parent or guardian check a student's fee status and pay outstanding fees online. It is provided as a convenience alongside, not a replacement for, the School's office records.

03Accurate information

The admission number and mobile number used for lookup should belong to the student concerned. Please contact the School office if you are unsure of these details, rather than guessing repeated combinations.

04Fee amounts & payments

The amount due shown on the portal is fetched securely from the School's records at the time of your visit and is locked server-side before checkout — it cannot be edited through the page or a shared link.

All payments are processed through Razorpay. Once a payment is confirmed by Razorpay and verified by our server, it is reflected against the student's fee record; this may take a short time to appear.

05Failed or duplicate payments

Occasionally a payment may fail after the amount has been debited, or the same fee may be paid more than once. Please see our Refund Policy for how these situations are handled.
